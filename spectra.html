What changes for your operations team

Nothing slips a season

Receivables aging is visible by scheme, agency and period, so an unfiled claim surfaces before the window closes.

A circular change is configuration

A revised mid-year circular becomes a new version your team configures, dry-runs and approves — not a vendor ticket.

Short pays explained

A lump-sum receipt is reconciled record by record, so every shortfall is attributed to the accounts it came from.

No double claiming

The same scheme, region and period cannot be claimed twice, and a retried call cannot credit a customer twice.

What's inside

Three stages of a claim season, one platform.

Configure & simulate

Scheme versioning

Eligibility rules, rates, caps and claim formats held as a version with an effective date — never edited in place.

Dry-run sandbox

Run a new version against real portfolio data to see what it would pay before it is activated. Nothing simulated is claimable.

Generate & file

Maker-checker batches

Claim batches are prepared by one officer and approved by another, with a required-document gate before submission.

Government-format export

Validated, immutable claim files in the format the agency prescribes, with the version that produced them recorded.

Reconcile & release

Record-level reconciliation

Lump-sum reimbursements matched down to the individual account, with rejections routed to a resolution path.

Governed credit release

Customer credits released only post authorisation; a clawback posts as a linked reversal, never an edit.

The boundary

SPECTRA never credits a customer on its own.

Security & deployment →

Eligibility and entitlement are computed by rule, to the paisa, against the scheme version in force on that date.

Nothing produced in the dry-run sandbox is claimable — simulation and production are separate by construction.

Customer credits are released only after authorisation, and a retried instruction cannot credit twice.

A clawback or correction posts as a new linked entry. Filed claims and past receipts are never overwritten.
