How we build

Four positions that decide what we ship and what we refuse to.

Arithmetic is not a judgement call

Anything that becomes a rupee figure is computed by a rule an officer can read, not inferred by a model.

The limits are the product

We state what the software will not do — move money, replace your core, decide on its own — because that is what gets a risk committee to yes.

Configuration over change requests

A new loan product, scheme version or payout grid is assembled by your team, not quoted as a vendor project.

Built for the audit, not the demo

Versioning, maker-checker and append-only records are in the foundation of every product, not added when an auditor asks.

What we work on

Three products, one engineering standard.

Each product is a separate codebase, separately deployed and licensed, and bought on its own. What they share is the standard: deterministic computation, maker-checker throughout, versioned configuration and an append-only audit log.
