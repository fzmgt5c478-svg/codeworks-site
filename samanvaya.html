What changes for finance and operations

Payout grids owned by the business

Slab and tiered rates are configuration your product team edits, dry-runs and submits for approval — not code.

A failing test case blocks the run

Grids carry test cases. If a case does not produce its expected payout, the version cannot be approved.

Clawbacks actually recovered

Early closure and fraud triggers post against the originating payout, and recovery is tracked to completion.

Books that tie every day

Commission expense, GST and TDS and recoveries post to a double-entry subledger that proves to the paisa.

What's inside

Prove it, govern it, settle it — then recover what is owed back.

Prove

Business-owned payout grids

Tiered and slab rates, product and geography splits, effective dates — all held as a version with an audit trail.

Test cases and dry runs

Every grid version is proved against expected outcomes on real data before a rupee is committed.

Govern

Dual control end to end

Runs, releases, clawbacks and waivers each require a checker who is not the maker.

Caps that hold

Per-agent quarterly caps are enforced at computation, with headroom visible before a run is approved.

Settle & recover

Locked advice and status loop

An approved run produces a locked payment advice for your payment hub, and ingests its status back.

Clawback and recovery

Triggers post against the originating payout and net off future settlements until recovered.
