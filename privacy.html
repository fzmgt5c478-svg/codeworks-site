Privacy Policy.

What this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. We collect as little as we can: an enquiry form, and no tracking of any kind.

01

Who we are

Codeworks is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. We build software for banks and NBFCs; this notice covers only the personal data collected through this website, not data processed inside a customer’s own deployment of our products.

Registered office: [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Mumbai, India. Written queries: sales@codeworks.ind.in.

Where we run our software inside your institution’s perimeter, your institution remains the data fiduciary for the borrower and customer records held there. We do not receive, store or process those records on this website or on our own systems.

02

What this website collects

The enquiry form asks for your name, institution, work email, role, what you need, and any context you choose to add. Every field is optional except those you decide to complete, and we ask for work rather than personal contact details on purpose.

Your theme preference (light or dark) is stored in your own browser under cw-theme. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

We set no cookies, and run no analytics, advertising or visitor-tracking of any kind on this site.

03

Why we collect it, and on what basis

We use enquiry details for one purpose: to reply to you and, if you want one, to arrange a scoping workshop or send the executive brief you asked for. We process them on the basis of the consent you give when you submit the form, which you may withdraw at any time.

We do not sell personal data, do not share it for anyone else’s marketing, and do not use it to build a profile of you or your institution.

04

Who else can see it

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server information including your IP address in order to serve the page.

Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page renders. No other third party receives anything from this site.

Enquiries reach us by email and are handled by the people who need to answer them. We may be required to disclose personal data where the law obliges us to.

05

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as the conversation is live and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that we can pick up where we left off if you return. Where an enquiry does not lead to a relationship, we delete it once it is clear there is nothing further to discuss.

If you ask us to erase your details sooner, we will, unless we are required to retain them.

06

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we have processed it; ask us to correct or complete anything inaccurate; ask us to erase it; nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to; and withdraw consent you previously gave.

To exercise any of these, write to sales@codeworks.ind.in. We will respond within the period the Act requires.

07

Grievances

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, our grievance officer is [GRIEVANCE OFFICER NAME], reachable at [GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. We aim to acknowledge a grievance within seventy-two hours.

If we do not resolve it to your satisfaction, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

08

Changes

If we change how this site handles personal data, we will update this page and change the date below. Material changes affecting data we already hold will be notified to the people concerned.
