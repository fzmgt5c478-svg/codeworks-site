Two capabilities, kept apart on purpose

The agents interpret. The engine computes. Neither does the other's job, which is what makes an answer defensible.

Agentic AI

Specialised agents, not one assistant

An agent per task — read a statement, search a registry, reconcile a schedule — rather than one general model asked to do everything.

Retrieval with a citation on every claim, back to the page, row or clause it came from.

Drafts memos, letters and monitoring narratives for an officer to edit and sign.

Agents never compute a figure. Interpretation only.

Model-agnostic — your choice of LLM, with personal data masked before processing.

Deterministic engine
