Three layers, kept apart on purpose

Neither layer does the other’s job. That is the whole design.

Layer 01

Deterministic engine

Rules configured by your operations team, not coded by a vendor on a ticket. Thresholds bound to a specific loan product, scheme or payout grid — never a global default. Every figure carries a full calculation trace, step by step.

Layer 02

Agentic AI analysts

An agent per task — read a statement, search a registry, reconcile a schedule — rather than one general assistant asked to do everything. Retrieval with a citation on every claim. Drafts memos, letters and narratives for officer review, and never computes a figure.

Layer 03

Officer decides and signs

Your delegation matrix enforced in software: quorum approvals, deviation capture, resubmission. The platform instructs; it never executes. A named person owns every decision it records.
