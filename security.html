The control story

Deployment

Private cloud or on-premise, inside your security perimeter. One isolated database per institution — never shared tenancy for financial data.

Personal data

Masked by default and before AI processing, restored after. Unmasking is permissioned, reasoned and audited.

Access

Single sign-on with your identity provider, role-based access, and maker-checker on every administrative action.

Records

All records and files encrypted at rest, with an append-only audit log that no role can edit.

Integration

Files and interfaces you already have. Plugs alongside your LOS and core banking — no rip-and-replace.

Model handling

Model-agnostic, with inference configured to your policy. Agents receive masked data and produce no figures.

What an auditor can ask for

The questions that usually take weeks, and where the answer lives.

Why was this decided this way?

The calculation trace for that case, plus the configuration version in force on that date.

Who approved it?

The maker, the checker, the timestamps and the channel — UI, API, batch or scheduled job.

What changed in the rules?

Every rule and grid change as a dual-approved version, with its effective date and its author.

Would it decide the same today?

The case replays against its own pinned configuration, independent of what is current.

The record

Every channel writes to one log.

UI, API, batch, webhooks, scheduled jobs and CLI all land in the same append-only record. A correction posts as a new linked entry, so the original never disappears.
